# Research dump

A running list of links to compiler designs, papers, posts and repos that are relevant to this repo. It has two tables of leads:

1. Leads that do not have an entry yet, and still need to be investigated.
2. Leads that were considered and declined.

When a lead gets an entry, delete its row: the entry's `README.md` and `FIDELITY.md` record the source, the pinned commit and what was read. New leads usually arrive as issues, see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

The "Read" column says whether the lead has been read in enough depth to rely on, not just listed. ✅ means read, ❌ means not read yet. A ✅ says what was read and what was not, because it often means one part of a larger source. Many ✅ rows were read by research subagents on 2026-11-10, and their claims were not re-checked by hand. The "What it could become" column holds a verdict on whether the design adds anything over the existing entries, and rows that add nothing move to the declined table.

| Wren | [wren-lang/wren](https://github.com/wren-lang/wren) at `99d2f0b8` (Robert Nystrom is the first name in `AUTHORS`, and the repo now lives under the `wren-lang` organization) | ✅ | Read in part: `wren_opcodes.h`, `callSignature` and `signatureSymbol` in `wren_compiler.c`, the call and return handlers of the interpreter loop in `wren_vm.c`, and `runFiber` in `wren_core.c`. ## Considered and declined

These do not pass "What earns an entry" in the [README](README.md).

| Lead | Why declined |
|------|--------------|
| A language that only lowers to LLVM IR | The back end would repeat `distilled/clang`. The front end, a language-specific lowering, is the part this repo leaves out. A design whose middle IR is the interesting part, such as MIR in rustc, is judged on that IR instead. |
| Bantam | A Pratt parser demo. The repo leaves out the parser. |
| Brainfuck compilers | Eight commands map nearly one-to-one onto machine code. There is no IR, scoping or type system, so the compiler is a table. `original/naive` already shows expanding code with no IR in between. |

## Cells of the design space map that will stay empty

The map at the top of the [README](README.md) has a crossed cell where this repo has decided not to add an entry, ever. A cell that is merely empty is a gap, not a decision, so only cells with a reason that holds for every design in them are crossed.

| Cell | Why it stays empty |
|------|--------------------|
| Analysis only, nothing in between | There is no representation to analyze, so the cell has no designs in it. |
| Analysis only, tree (AST) | Type checkers, linters and similar checks of source are front-end work, which this repo leaves out (it is also why Idris 2 is declined). |
